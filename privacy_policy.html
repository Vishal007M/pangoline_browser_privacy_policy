Privacy Policy

Pangolin Browser by Cyclushub

🔒 Your privacy is our priority
👋
Introduction

Welcome to Pangolin Browser, developed by Cyclushub. We built this browser with one goal: to give you a fast, private, and secure browsing experience — without selling your data or tracking your habits.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the controls you have over your data. By using Pangolin Browser, you agree to this policy.

🛡️
Data We Do NOT Collect

Pangolin Browser does not collect, transmit, or share the following:

  • ✕ Your browsing history or visited URLs
  • ✕ Search queries you enter in the address bar
  • ✕ Passwords, form data, or login credentials
  • ✕ Cookies from the websites you visit
  • ✕ Location data, camera, or microphone access
  • ✕ Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • ✕ Personal information of any kind
💾
Data Stored Locally On Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device and is never sent to Cyclushub or any third party:

  • ✓ Browsing history (only if "Save History" is enabled in Settings)
  • ✓ Bookmarks you save
  • ✓ App preferences (theme, search engine, ad block toggle, etc.)
  • ✓ Web cache and cookies set by websites you visit
  • ✓ Files you download via the browser

💡 You can clear all locally stored data at any time from Settings → Clear Browsing History, Clear Cookies, or Clear Cache.

🕶️
Incognito Mode

When browsing in Incognito Mode, Pangolin Browser applies additional protections:

  • ✓ No history is saved for incognito sessions
  • ✓ Cookies and site data are not persisted after the session
  • ✓ Web cache is disabled during incognito browsing
  • ✓ Screenshots are blocked (secure flag applied)
  • ✓ Biometric authentication required to access incognito tabs

Note: Incognito mode does not make you anonymous to websites, your ISP, or network administrators. It only prevents the app from saving local data.

🚫
Ad Blocking

When Ad Block is enabled in Settings, Pangolin Browser intercepts and blocks network requests to known advertising and tracking domains.

This blocking happens entirely on your device. No list of blocked URLs is ever sent to Cyclushub or any external server.

🌐
Third-Party Websites

Pangolin Browser is a web browser — it loads content from third-party websites that have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites you visit.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every website you visit, especially before submitting personal information.
